section 02 · all the little things

THE REASONS WHY I LOVE YOU

♡ Your cuteness

Honestly, when I first fell for you, it wasn't just because of your looks. It was the way you talked to me and your small, cute gestures. They melted me every time. Whenever I see that cute side of you, no matter how angry I am or how much blah blah I say, you melt me and make me fall for you again and again. It can make my whole month.

♡ The way you care

I get sympathy from almost everyone, but I don't know how to explain it: whether your sympathy is soft or strong, it makes me feel like I have someone special who thinks about me, someone who cares about me. It hits me so deeply that I start feeling happier and everything feels a little more okay.

♡ Your childish side

I like it when, sometimes — only sometimes, samjhi aap? — you show your childish side. It changes my mood and my thoughts, and I love that side of you.

♡ The names you call me

Sometimes when you call me something special, like “sir ji” or “sweetheart.” One time, I remember you called me “baby.” I said it was cringe, but I really liked it. I was so happy that I didn't even know how to react.

There are infinite reasons
that I will love you, forever. ♡
